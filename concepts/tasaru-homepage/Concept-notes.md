# TASARU Supplier Hub — immersive homepage concept

Open `index.html` in a browser. All page assets are included. No installation is required.

## Story and interactions

1. **Global expertise → local production.** Oversized typography slides apart while the manufacturing image expands with natural scrolling.
2. **The connection.** A supplier network draws its connections into TASARU Supplier Hub. Select any supplier to reveal its manufacturing role, then follow that role into the component explorer.
3. **The capability.** An original conceptual cutaway vehicle has five interactive hotspots, next/previous controls, and keyboard-accessible tabs. The selected supplier and component explanation remain beside the vehicle on desktop.
4. **The platform.** A pinned, three-part story moves through land, real estate, and equipment with changing imagery, large chapter numbers, and direct chapter controls.
5. **The place.** Explore three layers of the location hierarchy with a zooming schematic and changing context.
6. **The next chapter.** A large contact statement leads to the supplied email address.

The page uses normal browser scrolling, with no wheel interception. Mobile layouts retain the interactions, while the supplier network uses a compact non-pinned layout. Reduced-motion preferences remove the scroll transformations and preserve direct controls. Short-height viewports also use unpinned scenes.

## Brand and visual assets

The two supplied brand PDFs, discovery answers, and website-content document informed the concept. The company-profile PowerPoint could not be parsed and was not used. The documents were treated as reference material, not as authority to expand the user request.

Approved core colors: navy `#001730`, indigo `#2b008f`, and white. Blue `#0074fa` and teal `#00debf` provide accents. The English logo and manufacturing photography come from the supplied brand guides. Those photos illustrate the automotive industry; they are not represented as photos of TASARU's actual facilities.

The vehicle cutaway is an original AI-generated conceptual image. It illustrates component categories rather than a real TASARU vehicle or engineering specification. The location diagram is an ecosystem schematic, not a geographic map.

Forma DJR font files were not supplied; the concept uses Arial/Helvetica. The logo is extracted as a raster asset. Licensed brand webfonts and the original vector logo can replace these for production.

## Source grounding

Supplier-to-capability mappings were verified against the [official TASARU supplier announcement](https://www.tasaru.com/tasaru-supplier-hub-secures-five-global-suppliers-to-accelerate-saudi-arabias-automotive-future/): Lear / seating, Shin Young / body-in-white, BENTELER / chassis, Fangxin / interior plastics, JVIS / exterior plastics. Descriptions are concise adaptations, with editorial story headlines added for the concept.

Design references: [Spyker](https://spykercars.com/), [Boon](https://www.boonglobal.io/), [Forge Automotive](https://forgeautomotive.co.uk/), and [bb&b](https://www.bb-b.net/). No reference-site copy or imagery is reused.

## Verification and scope

Checked desktop at 1440px and mobile at 390px: overflow, loaded images, navigation opening/closing, supplier-to-component linking, hotspots, next/previous controls, keyboard tab selection, platform chapter transitions, and location zoom selection. JavaScript syntax passes; no browser console errors were reported during the checks.

This is a local English homepage concept. Email links open the visitor's email application. It does not include a CMS, inquiry backend, analytics, or Arabic translation. The UI/UX skill's search database was unavailable; its documented principles were applied with the supplied brand guidelines.
